Key Vocabulary — Digital Ethics & Netiquette

Word / Phrase Meaning (Indonesian) Context in Dialogue
Netiquette Tata krama / etika berinternet Rules or manners guiding polite and responsible online behavior.
Offensive language Bahasa kasar / menyinggung Words that insult or hurt others in online chats or comments.
Misunderstanding Kesalahpahaman Happens when facial expressions and tone of voice cannot be seen or heard.
Fake news Berita bohong / hoaks False information that spreads quickly and misleads many people.
Reliable sources Sumber tepercaya Credible references used to verify information before sharing.
Give credit Mencantumkan kredit / sumber asli Acknowledging the original creator of an article, photo, or video.
Privacy & dignity Privasi dan martabat Protecting personal data, passwords, and photos from unauthorized sharing.
Digital citizens Warga digital People who use the internet responsibly and ethically every day.
